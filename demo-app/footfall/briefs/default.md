Tiny Tasks is a small to-do list. You are already signed in, and your list has a dozen tasks on it.

Use it the way you would use any to-do list. Add a task, with a due date and without one. Mark a task done and mark it not done again. Search for a task you can see on the list. Delete a task you no longer need. Open the settings page and look through it. Sign out and sign back in if you want to see what that is like.

Judge it as the person you are, on the device you have. Say what got in your way, what you could not read, reach or tap, and what you did not understand. Do not report what works well.

The sign-in page accepts the username and password you were given, and nothing else.

Leave out how the app would look on a device you do not have, and how fast it loads.
